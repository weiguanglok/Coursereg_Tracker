⚖️ Official NUS CourseReg Priority Scoring & Tie-Breakers

When course demand exceeds available places, CourseReg allocates seats using each applicant's Priority Score. If scores are tied, official tie-breakers determine the final allocation in strict descending order.

Official Scoring Rubric: Priority Score = A (Programme) × B (Seniority) × C (Rank)
Category A
Programme Requirements
Descending order of points:
  1. UTown / NUSC courses
  2. Major core & Primary Major 1st Specialisation
  3. Primary Major courses
  4. Second Major Specialisation courses
  5. Faculty Requirement courses
  6. Second Major courses
  7. Restricted / Direct Minor courses
  8. Unrestricted Elective (UE) / General Education (GE)
Category B
Student Seniority
Descending order of points:
  1. Year 4 / Graduating Year 3 (Highest points)
  2. Year 3
  3. Year 2
  4. Year 1 (Freshmen)
Students closer to graduation are awarded higher point multipliers.
Category C
Rank Preference of Courses
Descending order of points:
  1. Rank 1 (Highest points)
  2. Rank 2
  3. Rank 3
  4. Rank 4
  5. Rank 5
  6. Rank 6
  7. Rank 7
  8. Rank 8 (Lowest points)
Rank your highest-priority / high-competition courses at Rank 1.
🎯 Official Undergraduate Tie-Breakers (In Descending Order of Consideration)

Evaluated when applicants have the exact same Priority Score (A × B × C):

1
Minimum Workload Shortfall: Students who have not yet attained their minimum workload are prioritized first.
2
Home Faculty Match: Student's home faculty is the course host faculty.
3
Carded National Athletes: Recognized national athletes (w.e.f. Sem 2, AY2022/23).
4
Course Feedback Points: Earned by submitting module feedback at the end of each semester.
5
Course Planning Exercise (CPEx) Points: Participation points from prior CPEx rounds (w.e.f. Sem 1, AY2021/22).
6
Random Balloting: Computerized randomized selection (final resort when all criteria above are tied).
⚠️
Why UEs / GEs face high Quota Exceeded rates: Unrestricted Electives and General Education courses sit at the bottom of Category A (Item 8). A junior bidding for a course as a UE will almost always have a lower Priority Score than majors or minors, regardless of their rank preference.
⭐
Free Tie-Breaker Advantage: Always complete Course Feedback and CPEx exercises! If you tie in Priority Score with another student in your cohort, these points secure the seat before random balloting is invoked.
